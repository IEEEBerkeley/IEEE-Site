About the Open House

Previously known as the HOPE & APE Showcase, the FA26 IEEE Open House will function similarly as a symposium with participants from all three IEEE DeCals: HOPE, APE, and Micromouse. This semester, along with our wonderful friends at Apple, we'll be opening the showcase (hence: Open House) to our fellow Berkeley students and engineers from our new industry sponsors!

Location: Grimes Engineering Center
Date: December 11th
